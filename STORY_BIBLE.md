## Part 1 — Canon (designer)

### The man

- **He's a corporate employee** who comes home from his job one late evening, like always. He's **approaching his 30s**.
- **He took the job under pressure** from family, friends and society, and from how the world worked when he was younger. **He hates it.** It's truly soul-sucking.
- **He wasn't always like this.** He used to have dreams. He was once **high-achieving**, had a **girlfriend**, his **health** and his **relationships**. It all slowly tumbled down because he couldn't fit this society and failed in its system. There can be **slight mentions of ADHD**.
- **The world started going to shit slowly**, with parallels to what's happening in our real world now. With **AI taking over**, he couldn't even understand what to do anymore.
- **He's addicted:** substances, porn, doomscrolling, mindless entertainment.
- **The job is breaking him down**, mentally and physically.
- **His family:**
  - He's been **disconnected from them for a long time**, since he started the job.
  - His **parents are ill** and live with his **younger brother**, who is "more built for this society".
  - His family life is a disaster.
- **His relationships are a disaster.** He always has **tons of unread messages**, and most people have **stopped reaching out**.
- **He lives in nostalgia** of what was.
- **He has no one but his mother** and a few people who would cry if he died, but he thinks they despise him now. **His guilt keeps him in the loop.**
- **He wants to die but is too much of a coward to do anything about it.** He isn't even scared anymore.
- **The house:** he got it because it was **cheap and far away**, but it's **near factories**, so the pollution will probably kill him.

### The night (corrections to `STORY_FLOW.md`)

- **The flow in `STORY_FLOW.md` is nearly correct.** Add more story, and use **environmental storytelling** to convey all of the above.
- **The calls are from the boss.** The man has **accidentally brought home a super important file from the office.** He finds it while taking things out of his bag: *"Huh… what the hell is this?"*
- **The main entrance is the west porch.**

### Ending — Horror Showdown (wake up **fed up**, at 100%)

1. He **pulls out a gun** and **everything turns dark.**
2. He sees only **an image of the ghost**, and follows it.
3. He takes **the last shot**, and it was **his boss, at the gate**, whom he shot. **The boss is dead.**
4. **The house is wrecked, and the ghost is still there.**

**Phone answered vs ignored:**
- **Answered:** at the end, **the ghost turns into the boss**, he shoots, and then realises it was really his boss.
- **Ignored:** **the ghost doesn't transform**, but he still shoots him.

### Ending — Ghost Burial (wake up **calm**, below 100%)

1. He really wants to get rid of this ghost, so he **starts chanting a mantra.** The ghost **runs away**.
2. He **follows its trail** to the **top-right area of the upper floor** (see the designer's image below).
3. The ghost **vanishes into the wall on the right.** There's a **crack** there. He realises it's a **fake wall**.
4. He gets an **axe** and **breaks it down.**
5. Behind it, he goes through the **vertical narrow space**, sifting through, and comes to **a tunnel that goes down**, near **the bottom corner triangle**.
6. It looks like a **dark void with darkness pouring out.**
7. It's a tunnel he has to **crawl** through. The "ground" tunnel is actually a **vertical vent**: the house floor and the vent meet at **90 degrees**. It works like a **portal**.
8. He crawls through to **another world**: a **dark sky**, **stone foundations**, and **broken fragments of the ghost's life**.
9. **The ghost's story:**
   - He was a **depressed person** who slowly distanced himself.
   - He **destroyed his life in this same house** and **committed suicide**.
   - **He just wanted a friend.** He wanted to be **seen, understood and loved**.
10. Towards the end, he finds **the ghost's body** in the other world and **burns it**, **promising** the ghost that he will **live**, to fulfil the ghost's wish to live, while he still has his life.

### Tone and presentation

- **Cutscenes,** in the spirit of **Cry of Fear, Max Payne and Silent Hill.** Some are **absurd**.
- **The game plays on absurd, deep and scary.** It doesn't take itself too seriously, but it still **touches the player** and lets the story pass through them. Players should **recognise how similar it is to a lot of their own lives.**
- **He talks to himself**, like B.J. in **Wolfenstein: The New Order**.
- **Some of Mr. Robot** in it.

### Approved additions (designer approved all of Claude's proposals, 23 Oct 2026)

- **The gun** is kept in his bedside drawer. It flashes past once in the Phase 2 drawer scare.
- **The axe** is in the store room, seen early.
- **The mantra is his mother's.** The player first hears it in her unheard voicemail.
- **Call #4 ends with the boss:** *"Fine. I'm coming over to get it myself."* That's why he's at the gate.
- **Burial ending card by phone:**
  - **Answered:** the boss's voicemail says *"…take a few days off."*
  - **Ignored:** *"…don't bother coming in."*
- **He talks to the player** (the Mr. Robot touch) as an imaginary friend, paid off in the Burial: *"I see you. I'm here."*
- **Environmental storytelling props** as listed in `STORY_FLOW.md` v2:
  - the climbing unread counter, with voicemails from Mom, his brother and his ex
  - the taped-up trophy box and the broken guitar
  - the unopened ADHD prescription
  - AI-layoff news, and a calm channel that's home video of him as a kid
  - the ghost's world mirroring his own objects, older: the ghost is who he's becoming
- **Three cutscene styles:**
  - in-engine cinematic (Silent Hill / Cry of Fear)
  - Max Payne-style comic panels with voiceover
  - absurd mini-scenes (file + choir, toast opera, kettle in the boss's voice, the cat judging you)
- **Player care:** a content note at launch and a support-line card in the credits.
