## Who you are

🟢 **A man pushing 30 with a corporate job he hates.** He took it because everyone said he should. He was once the kid with dreams: top marks, a girlfriend, his health, friends. Then the world slowly went sideways. AI ate the future he'd planned for, he couldn't keep up with a system that never fit his brain (a little ADHD, never treated), and everything slid. Now it's:
- substances, porn and doomscrolling
- hundreds of unread messages, and people who've stopped trying
- ill parents living with his younger brother, the one "built for this world"

He lives in nostalgia and guilt. He half-wants to die and hates himself for not being brave enough to. He bought this house because it was cheap and far away. The factories next door will probably kill him, and he doesn't care.

🟢 **The game never lectures.** It's **absurd, deep and scary at once.** It doesn't take itself too seriously, but the player should keep catching their own life in his.

🟢 **He talks to himself** in a quiet, bitter inner voice (Wolfenstein: The New Order), with **some Mr. Robot** in it.
- 🟡 **Mr. Robot touch:** he sometimes talks **to you, the player**, like an imaginary friend he isn't sure is there. *"You're still here? …Huh. Nobody stays."* It pays off in the Burial ending: the ghost only wanted a friend too.

## Prologue: Coming home (west porch) 🟢

Late evening. Factory chimneys breathe orange smog over the street. He climbs the **west porch** steps of a house too big for his salary.
- 🟡 *Cutscene: Max Payne-style comic panels, his voice over the commute.* *"Nine hours of making a chatbot sound human. It's better at it than me."*
- 🟡 **His phone buzzes; he doesn't look.** The lock screen reads **"212 unread."**

He unlocks the front door and steps into the living room.

> **Secret branch** 🟢 (rules from `MACRO_PLAN.md`): he walks straight upstairs and lies down **before starting any task**. *"Maybe ignoring it all was the real answer."* The house picks the **loop** or **the ghost wins** at random. The cat never came, so the cat rule doesn't apply.

## Phase 1: The evening routine

### 1. Drop your things, and the file 🟢
He hangs his jacket and empties his bag. Out slides a thick folder stamped **CONFIDENTIAL**. *"Huh… what the hell is this?"* It's the boss's super-important file, brought home by accident.
- 🟡 *Absurd cutscene: slow dolly-in on the folder, a choir hits one note, cut to his deadpan face.*
- As he walks away, **the jacket twitches or falls**, silently.

### 2. Make toast
Timing bar. Success **[−5]**. Burnt, jammed or launched **[+10]**. Two fails in a row sets off the alarm **[+3/s]**. Toast left 15 s gets eaten by something **[+10]**.
- 🟡 *Absurd cutscene for the first launch: slow-motion toast arcing across the kitchen to opera, landing butter-side down on the ceiling fan.*

### 3. Take a bath
Clothes slide off the hooks; directional prompts to snatch them back. Each lost item **[+6]** *"…Not again."* All saved **[−10]**.
- 🟡 *Mirror over the sink: for a second the reflection is younger, healthier, and smiling.*

### 4. Clean up the mess
Clutter to bins. Cleaned spots get messy again; an item flies back after three good drops. Above 40% the ghost snatches items **[+6]** and giggles **[+2]**. A clean room for 30 s **[−10]**.
- 🟡 *The clutter is his life:* energy drinks, takeout boxes, an unopened ADHD prescription, a crumpled gym membership card, a birthday card from Mom he never opened.

### 5. Watch TV
Static and horror **[+7]**, infomercials **[+5]**, rare calm **[−10]**, TV total capped at +10. Spam the remote and the batteries go under the couch **[+10]**. Above 50%, a ghost hand tug-of-war **[+5]**.
- 🟡 *The channels are the world going wrong:* a news anchor on AI layoffs, an infomercial for a "productivity supplement", a doomscroll feed, and the calm channel, which is **old home video of him as a kid**.

### 6. Phone call #1: the boss 🟢
The phone rings. It's **his boss, about the file.** Garbled, polite: *"You left something on…"*
- Answer **[−5]**, or ignore **[+2]**, and it goes to the log.

### 7. The cat, visit #1
A cat walks in. He doesn't own a cat. Pet it **[−20]**, and the screen softens. Ignore it, and it walks off into the hallway. The night remembers.
- 🟡 *Monologue:* *"Cats are smart. They don't stay where they're not loved."*

### Falling asleep
On the couch, mid-channel, the lights dim and the sound warps. 🟡 *Cutscene: the TV static becomes his ceiling, and the home video plays one last frame.*

## Phase 2: The wake-up

He wakes on the couch. It's colder and darker, with fog and whispers. 🟡 **His phone torch** is his only light, and it flickers by itself.

The **ghost director** spaces out the scares, always followed by quiet:
- the late reflection
- a door slam with fog spilling out
- the TV turning itself on
- the swinging jacket
- 🟡 the **drawer object-swap**, where the bedside drawer shows **a gun** for a heartbeat, then it's gone
- footsteps that stop when you turn
- a truck or train roaring past the window
- dolls
- a fake ring that chases you
- photos rotting over the night
- red eyes in dark corners

**Calm moments:** the juice box **[−5]**, and the radio's lo-fi **[−5]**. 🟡 *The radio's song is the one from his old relationship.*

**Grab a snack.** The fridge sneers *"Back so soon?"*, food slides away, a ghost hand yanks it, the door slams **[+5/+10/+3]**, success **[−5]**.

**Phone call #2, the boss again** 🟢: the phone is somewhere new. 🟡 *"I need that file first thing. Are you even listening?"*

## Phase 3: The haunted night

**Make Tea.** Ingredients vanish, you can carry two at a time, the power cuts, they re-scatter, junk lands on the stove, and you dodge for 20 s.
- Three hits: **fire [+15]**.
- Success: **tea [−10]**.
- 🟡 *Absurd beat:* the kettle screams in the boss's voice.

**Appliance chaos.** Hold to shut things off. Too slow **[+10]**. The ghost covers the switch **[+7]**; scare it off with the torch **[−5]**.

**Phone calls #3 and #4, the boss** 🟢: mid-chaos, warped. 🟡 *#4 ends with:* **"Fine. I'm coming over to get it myself."**

**The cat, visits #2 and #3:** on the counter, under the table, on the fridge. Above 60% it may hiss away in smoke (retry after 30 s). Petted **[−20 / −10]**: the house goes quiet.

> **Cat rule** 🟢: never petted all night means death in your sleep.

## The final sleep

Bed, red light, the blanket lifting, ghost arms in the fog **[+15 / −20 / +10 once]**. Then: **[Stay asleep]** or **[Wake up]**.

## Every ending

**The cat, the phone, and how you wake** decide it.

### 💀 You die in your sleep (cat never petted)
Any phone, any choice. The ghost settles over you, and you don't wake.

### 🔁 The loop (cat ✔, phone answered, stay asleep)
The alarm rings. Same evening, same bag, same toaster. Job, home and ghost all stay.

### 😠 Unemployed, ghost angry (cat ✔, phone ignored, stay asleep)
A voicemail in the morning: fired. The house stands, and the ghost is furious.

### 🔫 Horror Showdown 🟢 (cat ✔, wake at 100%)
He wakes **fed up** and **pulls out a gun.** Everything turns dark: Max Payne's blood-trail level meets Cry of Fear. Only **an image of the ghost** remains, flickering ahead, and he follows it through the wrecked house, firing. **The last shot.** Then the dark lifts: he shot **his boss, at the gate.** The boss came for the file, and he's dead. The house is destroyed. **The ghost is still there.**
- **Phone answered** 🟢: at the very end **the ghost visibly turns into his boss** before the final shot. He realises after.
- **Phone ignored** 🟢: **no transformation.** He still shoots him.

### 🕯 Ghost Burial 🟢 (cat ✔, wake below 100%)
He wakes **calm**, and he wants this thing gone.
1. **He starts chanting a mantra.** The ghost **flees**.
2. **He follows its trail upstairs** to the **north-east corner** of the upper floor. The ghost **vanishes into the right-hand wall**.
3. **A crack. A fake wall.** He fetches an **axe** and smashes through.
4. Behind it is a **narrow space between the walls.** *(Verified in the real house: about 0.6 m wide, running the whole east side.)* He **squeezes sideways** south through it.
5. In the **bottom-right corner**, the space opens into the **triangle**: *(verified: a hollow shaft with no floor on either level, falling 6.7 m to the foundation)*. Darkness pours out of it.
6. He **crawls in.** The tunnel turns **90° from the floor**, a **vertical vent**, and the world rotates with him. **It's a portal.**
7. **The other world:** a dark sky, stone foundations, and **fragments of the ghost's life** scattered like ruins. 🟢 He was a depressed man who drifted away from everyone, wrecked his life **in this same house**, and **took his own life**. **He just wanted a friend, someone to see him, understand him, love him.**
8. He **finds the ghost's body** and **burns it**, **promising** to live, for both of them, while he still has his life.

- 🟡 **Mr. Robot payoff:** the friend the ghost wanted… *"I see you. I'm here."* (the line he's been saying to you all night).
- 🟡 **Phone answered vs ignored:** the same journey. The final card differs: answered, the boss's voicemail says *"…take a few days off"*; ignored, *"…don't bother coming in."* **← needs your call**

### 🤫 Secret ending
Bed before any task: the loop, or the ghost wins.

## 🟡 Proposals waiting for your yes

### Where things come from
1. **The gun:** kept in his bedside drawer. *(Quietly tied to his death wish; it flashes past once in the Phase 2 drawer scare, so the Showdown doesn't come from nowhere.)*
2. **The axe:** in the **Storeroom** (upstairs, beside the Locked Room), seen early in the Storeroom blackout. *(See `STORY_BIBLE.md` and `DESIGN_LOGIC.md` #55–56.)*
3. **The mantra:** **his mother's**, the one she said over him when he was sick as a kid. You hear it once, early, in her **unheard voicemail**. *(It ties the Burial to family, which is the thing he lost.)*
4. **The boss at the gate:** he came for the file. Call #4 sets it up: *"I'm coming over."*

### Environmental storytelling (all cheap props)

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **Porch** | Smog and factory glow; an air-quality warning on a flyer; mail piled up (final notices, a wedding invite he never answered) |
| **Phone** | The unread counter climbs all night; unplayed voicemails: **Mom** (the mantra), the **brother** ("Mom's asking about you"), the **ex** (old) |
| **Living room** | A box of **trophies and certificates** taped shut; an old **guitar** with a broken string; **framed photos** that rot through the night |
| **Kitchen** | Fridge photos of a younger him with friends; an AI-layoff newspaper headline; energy drinks |
| **Bedroom** | The drawer; pill bottles; a laptop glowing with 40 open tabs and a half-written resignation letter |
| **Bathroom** | The younger reflection; a calendar stuck on the month things fell apart |
| **Ghost's world** | The same kinds of objects as his, but **older**: the ghost's trophies, the ghost's unread letters. *You realise the ghost is who he's becoming.* |

### Cutscene style (three kinds)
- **In-engine cinematic** (Silent Hill / Cry of Fear): wake-up, Showdown, Burial descent.
- **Comic-book panels with voiceover** (Max Payne): prologue, falling asleep, every ending card. *Quick to make and very distinct.*
- **Absurd mini-scenes:** file discovery, toast opera, kettle screaming in the boss's voice, the cat judging you.
- *In greybox, every cutscene is a placeholder (camera move + subtitles) so the whole story plays early; the real versions come in the art passes.*

### Care for players
- 🟡 The story deals with **depression and suicide**. I recommend a short **content note** at launch and a **support-line card** in the credits. It's common practice, and it protects the people this game is about.
